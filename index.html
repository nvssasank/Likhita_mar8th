Childhood Days

Neighbors, Nothing More

You lived right next door, just one year younger than me. We talked when we saw each other, played shuttle on our street during 10th class. But I was blind — I had no idea that the girl next door would one day become my entire world.

Inter Days - Same College

Cycling Together, Hearts Apart

When you joined Inter 1st year at my college, you'd wait and cycle with me every morning. I was too foolish to see what was right in front of me. Then one day, you proposed — indirectly, but I knew. And I... I said no. I hurt you. I'm so sorry for making you wait.

The Distance Years

Silent Hearts Reconnecting

After I rejected you, we grew distant. I went to B.Tech, you finished Inter. No calls, no messages. Then one day I saw your Snapchat story about your internship. I typed a message but didn't send it... but you saw I was typing. You called. That's when everything changed.

April 2024

The Bike Ride That Changed Everything

You came to meet me in Hyderabad. I picked you up on my Duke. When you put your hand on my waist, I held it with my left hand without thinking. We rode for 5 hours that night. That's when I realized — this isn't just friendship. I love you. The next morning at 8 AM, I came to your place and proposed properly.

June 2024

Pune - Our First Trip

We went to Pune with friends. The bike rides, the mountains, the laughter — everything was perfect. Every moment with you felt like a dream. That trip showed me what happiness really looks like: it looks like you, beside me.

March 8, 2025

I booked a room, brought cake and gifts. After cutting the cake, I got down on one knee and proposed to you with a rose — the proper way you always deserved. You smiled, I smiled, and that moment became my favorite memory of all time.

💌

March 24, 2025

My Birthday - Your Love Letter

You booked a room for my birthday. You gave me gifts and a beautiful love letter. We took so many pictures together. I've never been happier. Seeing the effort you put in, the love in your eyes — I fell for you all over again.

💔

April 2025

The Storm We Survived

My families found out. They said no. Everything fell apart. We fought, we cried, there was distance and silence. But you... you never left. You stayed loyal when I gave you every reason to walk away. You taught me what real love means: staying even when it's hard.

💼

September 2025

New Beginnings

I finally got a job in Bangalore. A new chapter began. You supported me through everything — the jobless days, the family issues, the uncertainty. You never complained. You just... loved me. Unconditionally.

💞

February 2026 - Now

Long Distance, Endless Love

We're miles apart but closer than ever. Yes, I irritate you sometimes — I talk to colleagues, I tease you, we have silly fights. But they last only an hour because you understand me like no one else. You're patient, loyal, and so incredibly loving. Distance makes it hard, but our calls, our chats, our romantic talks — they make everything worth it. You are my home, no matter where I am.

A Love Letter for You

My dearest love, There are moments in life when words feel too small to hold everything you feel. This is one of those moments. But I'll try anyway, because you deserve to know just how deeply I love you. Do you remember when we were just kids, playing shuttle on our street? You were the girl next door — literally right there, one house away. We'd talk, we'd laugh, but I never saw you the way I should have. I was blind. Completely, foolishly blind. Even when we cycled to college together during Inter, when you'd wait for me to start just so we could ride together, I still didn't get it. You were falling for me, and I... I had no idea I was missing the most beautiful thing that would ever happen to me. Then you proposed. Indirectly, sweetly, with all the courage in the world. And I rejected you. God, I rejected you. I think about that moment and my heart still breaks. I hurt the one person who would become my everything. I made you wait. I made you cry. I am so, so sorry for that. If I could go back, I would shake that younger version of me and tell him: "You fool, she's the one. Can't you see it?" After that, we drifted apart. Two years of silence. No calls, no messages. You went your way, I went mine. I thought that was it — that I'd lost something I didn't even know I had. But the universe wasn't done with us yet. That Snapchat story. Your internship. I typed a message and didn't send it, but you saw I was typing. You called me. Do you know what that call meant? It meant fate was giving me a second chance. And this time, I wasn't going to be stupid enough to waste it. We started talking again. Late-night calls, random chats, silly conversations about coding and life. I told myself we were just friends. But deep down, I knew. I was falling. Slowly, surely, completely. Then came April 2024. The night that changed my entire life. I came to you in Hyderabad to meet. I picked you up on my Duke, and when you climbed on behind me, something shifted in the universe. You put your hand on my waist — just a simple gesture, natural, nothing dramatic. But I... I couldn't help myself. I reached back with my left hand and held yours. We rode like that, through the streets, for five hours. Five hours that felt like five minutes. Five hours where I kept thinking, "This is it. This is what love feels like." I was so confused that night. "Am I in love? Is this real? What is happening to me?" I couldn't sleep. I talked to my sister. And finally, FINALLY, I understood. I loved you. I had always loved you. I just needed that bike ride, your hand in mine, to wake me up. The next morning at 8 AM, I came to you. I was nervous, excited, terrified. I proposed to you properly. And you said yes. You said yes, and in that moment, my entire world changed. I became the luckiest person alive. Then we went to Pune with friends. Those mountains, that bike, the wind in our faces, your laughter filling the air — I've never felt more alive. Every turn of the road, every view, every moment was beautiful because you were there. We created memories I'll carry with me forever. March 2025. Our birthdays. The most magical month of my life. March 8th was your day. I booked a room, brought a cake, gifts, and most importantly, a rose. After we cut the cake, I got down on one knee and proposed to you the way you always deserved — romantically, properly, with all my heart. Your smile in that moment? That's an image I'll never forget. That's the smile I want to see for the rest of my life. Then March 24th came. My birthday. You surprised me with gifts and a love letter that made me cry. We took so many pictures. We laughed, we hugged, we celebrated. I remember thinking, "This is happiness. This is what it means to be complete." But life isn't always kind, is it? April came. My family found out about us. They said no. Suddenly, the world we'd built together felt like it was crumbling. I had no job. I was stuck at home, doing part-time work, building websites just to keep busy, to keep from falling apart. I couldn't talk to you as much as I wanted. There was distance, silence, fear. But you? Oh, my love, you never left. I've never met anyone like you. You're loyal in a way I didn't know existed. When I couldn't call for days, you didn't complain. When I was irritable and distant, you stayed patient. When I hurt you with my carelessness — talking too much with colleagues, teasing you, forgetting how my actions affect you — you didn't walk away. You just... stayed. You loved me through it all. I don't deserve you. I really don't. But somehow, by some miracle, you chose me. And you keep choosing me, every single day. September brought change. I got a job in Bangalore. A new city, new opportunities, new challenges. But it also brought distance. Real, physical, painful distance. You're there, I'm here, and some days it's so hard I can barely breathe. I miss you. I miss your voice, your laugh, your presence. I miss the way you look at me. I miss everything(that also hehe). But even with the distance, we make it work. We talk every day. We fight sometimes — silly little fights that last barely an hour because we can't stay mad at each other. We laugh through video calls. We share our days, our dreams, our fears. You understand me better than anyone else in this world. And I love you. God, I love you so much. I love the way you're patient with me when I'm being difficult. I love how you never give up on us, even when things are hard. I love your loyalty, your kindness, your incredible heart. I love how you make me want to be a better person. I love how you believe in me even when I don't believe in myself. You've taught me what real love is. It's not just the romantic gestures or the sweet words. It's staying when it's hard. It's forgiving when it hurts. It's choosing each other every single day, even when life throws obstacles in your way. It's building a future together, one day at a time, with patience and faith. This website is my way of showing you what you mean to me. You gave me a love letter on my birthday, and I want to give you our entire story — every moment, every memory, from neighbors to soulmates. This is us. The good, the bad, the beautiful, the painful — all of it. And I wouldn't change a single thing, because it all led me to you. You are my home. When I'm in Bangalore, when you're wherever you are, it doesn't matter. Home isn't a place. Home is you. It's your voice on the phone at the end of a long day. It's your laugh when I make a stupid joke. It's your hand in mine, just like that night on the Duke. I don't know when our families will say yes. I don't know when the distance will end. I don't know when we'll finally be together without obstacles. But I know this, with every fiber of my being: I will wait for you. I will choose you. Every single day, for the rest of my life. And one day — one beautiful, perfect day — I will marry you. I will stand beside you and promise you forever, and I will mean every word. Until then, my love, know this: You are loved more than words can ever express. You are cherished beyond measure. You are my past, my present, and my future. You are my everything. Thank you for waiting for me to wake up and see what was right in front of me. Thank you for never giving up on us. Thank you for loving me even when I don't deserve it. Thank you for being you — the most beautiful person I've ever known. I love you. I love you. I love you. Always and forever.

Forever yours, with all my heart 💕
