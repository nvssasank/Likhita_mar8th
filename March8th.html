Our Love Story

Childhood Days

Neighbors, Nothing More

You lived right next door, just one year younger than me. We talked when we saw each other, played shuttle on our street during 10th class. But I was blind — I had no idea that the girl next door would one day become my entire world.

🚴

Inter Days - Same College

Cycling Together, Hearts Apart

When you joined Inter 1st year at my college, you'd wait and cycle with me every morning. I was too foolish to see what was right in front of me. Then one day, you proposed — indirectly, but I knew. And I... I said no. I hurt you. I'm so sorry for making you wait.

📱

The Distance Years

Silent Hearts Reconnecting

After I rejected you, we grew distant. I went to B.Tech, you finished Inter. No calls, no messages. Then one day I saw your Snapchat story about your internship. I typed a message but didn't send it... but you saw I was typing. You called. That's when everything changed.

🏍️

April 2024

The Bike Ride That Changed Everything

You came to meet me in Hyderabad. I picked you up on my Duke. When you put your hand on my waist, I held it with my left hand without thinking. We rode for 5 hours that night. That's when I realized — this isn't just friendship. I love you. The next morning at 8 AM, I came to your place and proposed properly.

🌄

June 2024

Pune - Our First Trip

We went to Pune with friends. The bike rides, the mountains, the laughter — everything was perfect. Every moment with you felt like a dream. That trip showed me what happiness really looks like: it looks like you, beside me.

🎂

March 8, 2025

Your Birthday - On One Knee

I booked a room, brought cake and gifts. After cutting the cake, I got down on one knee and proposed to you with a rose — the proper way you always deserved. You smiled, I smiled, and that moment became my favorite memory of all time.

💌

March 24, 2025

My Birthday - Your Love Letter

You booked a room for my birthday. You gave me gifts and a beautiful love letter. We took so many pictures together. I've never been happier. Seeing the effort you put in, the love in your eyes — I fell for you all over again.

💔

April 2025

The Storm We Survived

My families found out. They said no. Everything fell apart. We fought, we cried, there was distance and silence. But you... you never left. You stayed loyal when I gave you every reason to walk away. You taught me what real love means: staying even when it's hard.

💼

September 2025

New Beginnings

I finally got a job in Bangalore. A new chapter began. You supported me through everything — the jobless days, the family issues, the uncertainty. You never complained. You just... loved me. Unconditionally.

💞

February 2026 - Now

Long Distance, Endless Love

We're miles apart but closer than ever. Yes, I irritate you sometimes — I talk to colleagues, I tease you, we have silly fights. But they last only an hour because you understand me like no one else. You're patient, loyal, and so incredibly loving. Distance makes it hard, but our calls, our chats, our romantic talks — they make everything worth it. You are my home, no matter where I am.

Straight from my heart

A Love Letter for You

My dearest love, I'm writing this because words fall short when I try to tell you face-to-face. So here, in this space just for us, let me try. You were the girl next door. The one I was too blind to see. We played shuttle on our street, cycled to college together, and I still didn't understand what the universe was trying to tell me. When you proposed to me that day during Inter, I said no. I hurt you. I made you wait. I'm so sorry for that. I was foolish, confused, too young to recognize love even when it was staring me in the face. But you... you never gave up on me. Even after the rejection, after the silence, after years of no contact — somehow, we found our way back. That Snapchat message I almost sent, your call that followed, our late-night conversations while you were in Hyderabad and I was finishing B.Tech — that was fate giving us a second chance. April 2024. The night that changed everything. When you came to meet me and sat on my Duke, when you put your hand on my waist and I held it with my left hand without even thinking — that moment, I finally understood. This wasn't friendship. This was love. It always had been. The next morning, I came to you at 8 AM and properly asked you to be mine. You said yes. My heart has never been the same since. Then came Pune. Those mountain roads, that bike ride, your laughter echoing in the wind — that trip showed me what happiness really looks like. It looks like you, next to me, enjoying every moment we create together. Our birthdays in March 2025 were magical. On March 8th, I got down on one knee with a rose and proposed the way you always deserved. On March 24th, you surprised me with gifts and that beautiful love letter. We took countless pictures. We were so happy. We thought nothing could touch us. But then April came. My families found out. They said no. Everything fell apart. We fought, we cried, there was distance and silence. I had no job, I was stuck at home, and I could barely talk to you. I did part-time work, built websites, and counted the days until things would get better. That was the hardest time of my life. But you? You never left. You never blamed me. You never showed anger even when I gave you every reason to be upset. When I couldn't talk for days, you understood. When I was irritable and distant, you stayed patient. You are the most loyal person I've ever known. While I was busy creating unnecessary drama — talking to colleagues, making you jealous, being careless with your feelings — you just... loved me. Unconditionally. Patiently. Completely. September came and I got the job in Bangalore. A new chapter began. But with it came new challenges — long distance, busy schedules, different time zones, loneliness. And still, you stayed. We talk every day, we resolve our silly fights in an hour, we make each other laugh even through screens. You understand me better than anyone else in this world. I know I'm not perfect. I irritate you sometimes. I get too close to my colleagues and forget to think about how that makes you feel. I tease you and push your buttons just to see you react. But you... you handle it all with grace I don't deserve. You never leave. You never threaten to walk away. You just remind me to do better, and you love me through my mistakes. You've taught me what real love means. It's not just the butterflies and the romance. It's staying when it's hard. It's forgiving when it hurts. It's choosing each other every single day, even when life gives you a thousand reasons not to. This website is my gift to you. Because you gave me a love letter on my birthday, and I want to give you something even bigger — a space filled with our entire story, our memories, our journey from neighbors to soulmates. This is us. Every laugh, every tear, every bike ride, every fight, every makeup — it all matters. It all brought us here. You are my home. Whether you're next door or miles away in another city, you are where I belong. I don't know when our families will say yes. I don't know when the distance will end. But I know this: I will wait for you. I will choose you every single day. And one day — one beautiful, perfect day — I will marry you. Until then, know this: you are loved. You are cherished. You are my everything. Thank you for staying. Thank you for loving me. Thank you for being you.

Forever yours, with all my heart 💕
